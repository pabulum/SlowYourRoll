▸ How the math works

Paste a QE Live upgrade report and this ranks every raid boss and M+ dungeon you can bonus-roll by the expected value of one token:

EV = ( Σ score of items you still want ÷ items still in the pool ) ÷ token cost

Scores come straight from your report (QE Live for healers, your sim for DPS/tanks). Each item in a pool is one of three states — tap to cycle:

WantAn upgrade still in the pool. Its score counts toward EV.
OwnYou have it (or took it from your vault). It stays in the roll pool — rolling it would just dupe — but it's worth 0 to you. Items in your /simc at ≥ the ilvl a roll here would hand you auto-mark Own; a copy below that shows a have N tag and stays Want, because the roll is still an upgrade.
RolledYou bonus-rolled it — gone from the pool for good.

Zero-score items (no upgrade for you) still sit in the pool as filler and drag EV down — that's real. Raid difficulty switches which tier's item levels/scores are used. Token cost defaults to the season's: Override it per encounter in any expanded row.

Very rare items are rare off a boss kill — a bonus roll draws evenly from the pool, so they count here exactly like any other drop, and their EV is not discounted. That's usually a reason to chase one rather than to shy off it: the roll is the one place a very rare item costs the same as common filler. They're flagged ✦ in the recommendation up top.
