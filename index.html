▸ How the math works

Paste a QE Live upgrade report and this ranks every raid boss and M+ dungeon you can bonus-roll by the expected value of one token:

EV = ( Σ score of items you still want ÷ items still in the pool ) ÷ token cost

Scores come straight from your report (QE Live for healers, your sim for DPS/tanks). Each item in a pool is one of three states — tap to cycle:

WantAn upgrade still in the pool. Its score counts toward EV.
OwnYou have it (or took it from your vault). It stays in the roll pool — rolling it would just dupe — but it's worth 0 to you. Items in your /simc at ≥ the drop's ilvl auto-mark Own; a copy at a lower ilvl shows a have N tag and stays Want (roll it if that lower copy is a different, weaker track).
RolledYou bonus-rolled it — gone from the pool for good.

Zero-score items (no upgrade for you) still sit in the pool as filler and drag EV down — that's real. Raid difficulty switches which tier's item levels/scores are used. Token cost is 1 for everything in S2; set it to 2 to model an S1-style raid roll.

Very rare items have far lower drop odds than the uniform pool assumes, so treat their EV as a ceiling. Everything saves in this browser; Export gives you a backup file.
